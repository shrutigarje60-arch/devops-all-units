# Observe, Reflect, and Make (ORM): Design Thinking Framework

The **Observe, Reflect, and Make (ORM)** framework guides designers and innovators from ambiguity to tangible solutions through empathy, critical analysis, and iterative creation.

---

## 1. Observe Phase (Gathering Data)
Immersing in the user's world to gather raw, unbiased qualitative data about behaviors, needs, and context.

* **Key Objectives:**
  * Identify latent (unarticulated) user needs.
  * Understand the real-world context of problems.
  * Gather evidence without prematurely jumping to conclusions.
* **Core Techniques:**
  * **Shadowing:** Following users during their daily routines.
  * **Contextual Inquiry:** Interviewing users while they perform tasks in context.
  * **Fly on the Wall:** Unobtrusive observation from a distance.
* **Expert Tip:** Focus on **Extreme Users** (those using systems in unusual ways) to unlock deeper insights relevant to the wider population.

---

## 2. Reflect Phase (Synthesizing Insights)
Transforming raw data into meaningful patterns to transition from problem identification to solution strategy.

* **Key Objectives:**
  * Identify patterns and thematic clusters in data.
  * Formulate a clear **Problem Statement / Point of View (POV)**.
  * Deeply empathize with the user's emotional experience.
* **Critical Tools:**
  * **Affinity Mapping:** Grouping observations to isolate key pain points.
  * **5 Whys:** Root-cause analysis by iteratively probing deeper.
  * **Empathy Maps:** Categorizing what users **Said, Did, Thought, and Felt**.

---

## 3. Make Phase (Prototyping & Iteration)
Externalizing abstract ideas into tangible artifacts to drive feedback and rapid learning.

* **Key Objectives:**
  * Make abstract ideas visual and tangible.
  * Fail early and cheaply to minimize resource risk.
  * Solicit direct feedback via physical/digital artifacts.
* **Key Steps:**
  1. **Ideation:** High-volume brainstorming (prioritize quantity).
  2. **Low-Fidelity Prototyping:** Rapid builds using simple materials (paper, wireframes).
  3. **Testing:** Re-entering the *Observe* phase by testing prototypes with users.

---

## Process Overview

| Phase | Role | Goal | Mindset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Observe** | The Witness | Gather Evidence | Curious & Objective |
| **Reflect** | The Analyst | Generate Insights | Critical & Empathetic |
| **Make** | The Builder | Test Solutions | Experimental & Iterative |
