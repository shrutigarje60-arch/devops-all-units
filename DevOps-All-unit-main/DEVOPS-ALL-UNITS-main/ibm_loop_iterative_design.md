# Application of the IBM Loop in Iterative Design

## Overview
Enterprise Design Thinking (EDT) scales human-centered design for global impact by aligning teams around user needs and driving continuous iteration.

---

## Three Guiding Principles
* **User Outcomes:** Measure success by how effectively real user pain points and needs are fulfilled, rather than the number of features shipped.
* **Diverse Teams:** Engage inclusive, cross-disciplinary teams to break down organizational silos and generate broader ideas.
* **Restless Reinvention:** Treat all deliverables as prototypes and iterate continuously to maintain value and relevance.

---

## Core Engine: The IBM Loop
The IBM Loop represents a continuous, scalable, and action-oriented design cycle that guides teams from ambiguity to clarity:
* **Continuous:** Design effort extends beyond product launch.
* **Scalable:** Suitable for both small project teams and global enterprise environments.
* **Action-Oriented:** Translates complex problems into actionable solutions.

---

## The Three Phases of the Loop

### 1. Observe (Immersion)
Focuses on immersing in the real world to uncover user context and unstated needs.
* **User Research:** Conduct field interviews and shadowing.
* **Empathy Mapping:** Capture what users say, do, think, and feel.
* **Sponsor Users:** Continuously engage real users throughout the development lifecycle.

### 2. Reflect (Synthesis & Alignment)
Acts as a strategic pause for synthesizing observations and aligning stakeholders.
* **Synthesize & Align:** Share insights and agree on project directions.
* **Playback Sessions:** Conduct iterative playbacks to align stakeholders and identify gaps early.

### 3. Make (Creation & Prototyping)
Transforms abstract intent into tangible, testable assets to accelerate learning.
* **Low-Fidelity:** Utilize rapid paper sketches.
* **High-Fidelity:** Develop interactive wireframes.
* **MVP:** Deliver Minimum Viable Products for empirical testing
