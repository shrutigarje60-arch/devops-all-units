# Comprehensive Guide to Agile Methodology & Frameworks

**Agile** is an iterative and incremental mindset and project management approach designed to deliver value quickly through small, continuous releases rather than a single "big bang" launch.

---

## 1. Core Principles: The Agile Manifesto (2001)

Agile prioritizes four fundamental values over traditional linear (Waterfall) models:

* **Individuals and interactions** over processes and tools.
* **Working software** over comprehensive documentation.
* **Customer collaboration** over contract negotiation.
* **Responding to change** over following a plan.

---

## 2. Scrum Framework

Scrum is the most widely adopted Agile framework, designed for managing and sustaining complex product development.

* **Key Role — Product Owner:** Focuses on the *"What"*. Responsible for prioritizing and managing the **Product Backlog** to ensure maximum business value.
* **Sprint Structure:** Development is organized into fixed-length iterations (typically 1–4 weeks) where incremental features are built and delivered.

---

## 3. The Agile Lifecycle

Every sprint operates as a self-contained mini-project incorporating key ceremonial stages:

1. **Backlog Grooming:** Continuous refinement and prioritization of user stories and features in the backlog.
2. **Sprint Planning:** Defining the scope and committing to specific backlog items for the upcoming sprint.
3. **Daily Stand-up:** A daily 15-minute sync to align activities, evaluate progress, and clear blockers.
